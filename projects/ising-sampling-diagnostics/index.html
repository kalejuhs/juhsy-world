01 / LOCAL DYNAMICS

Each row-major site gets a 50% flip proposal; accepted flips follow the Metropolis probability. Local updates can mix slowly when large domains form.

02 / CLUSTER DYNAMICS

At h = 0 and k_B = 1, Wolff grows a same-spin cluster with bond probability 1 − exp(−2J/T), then flips the whole cluster.

03 / DIAGNOSTICS

Rank-normalized split R-hat, τ, and Bulk/Tail ESS are computed separately for signed m and |m|. Constant |m| is not assessable, not a pass. For either observable, the heuristic screen requires R-hat < 1.01 and total Bulk/Tail ESS ≥ 400 across 4 replicas; passing is not proof of equilibrium.
